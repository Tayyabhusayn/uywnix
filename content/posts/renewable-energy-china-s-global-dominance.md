China has firmly established itself as the leader in the global transition to renewable energy. From solar panels to electric vehicle batteries, Chinese technology is at the forefront of the green revolution.
### Solar and Wind Power
China produces a vast majority of the world's solar panels and wind turbines. Their ability to manufacture these at a lower cost has helped accelerate the adoption of clean energy in many developing nations.
### The EV Battery Market
The race for better electric vehicle (EV) batteries is currently being won by Chinese companies. They control much of the supply chain for raw materials like lithium and cobalt, making them essential partners for car manufacturers worldwide.
### International Competition
Western countries are trying to catch up by offering subsidies for local manufacturing. However, China's head start in technology and infrastructure makes it a difficult gap to close in the near future.
