Artificial Intelligence (AI) has already changed how we work and live. Now, the tech world is looking toward the next big step: Artificial General Intelligence (AGI).
### What is AGI?
Unlike the AI we use today, which is designed for specific tasks (like recognizing faces or translating languages), AGI would be able to learn and perform any intellectual task that a human can do. It is often described as the "holy grail" of computer science.
### Progress in 2026
In the past year, we have seen massive leaps in how AI understands complex reasoning. While we aren't at AGI yet, the systems are becoming much better at solving problems across different fields without needing special training for each one.
### Ethical Considerations
As AI becomes more powerful, the need for safety and regulation grows. Experts are working on ways to ensure that as we move closer to AGI, these systems remain helpful and aligned with human values.
