The situation in Myanmar remains one of the most complex conflicts in Southeast Asia. Since the military takeover in 2021, the country has faced ongoing internal strife, affecting millions of lives.
### The Current State of Conflict
Fighting between the military and various resistance groups continues across many regions. While the military maintains control over major cities, many rural areas are now governed by local groups or the National Unity Government (NUG).
### Humanitarian Impact
The biggest victims are the civilians. Thousands have been displaced, and access to basic needs like food and healthcare is limited in many conflict zones. International aid organizations are working hard, but reaching those in need is often dangerous.
### Global Response
The international community continues to debate the best way to help. Some countries have imposed sanctions, while others are calling for a peaceful dialogue. However, a clear solution remains out of reach as the conflict moves into another year.
