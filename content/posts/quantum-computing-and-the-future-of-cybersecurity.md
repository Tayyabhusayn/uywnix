As quantum computing moves from theory to reality, it presents both an opportunity and a threat to global cybersecurity. In 2026, the race to develop "quantum-resistant" encryption is in full swing.
### The Threat to Current Encryption
Current encryption methods, which protect everything from bank accounts to state secrets, could potentially be broken by powerful quantum computers. This has created an urgent need for new security standards.
### Developing New Standards
Governments and tech companies are working together to create new encryption algorithms that even a quantum computer cannot crack. These "post-quantum" standards are currently being tested and implemented across critical infrastructure.
### A More Secure Future?
While the transition is challenging, it also provides an opportunity to build a more robust and secure internet. The lessons learned during this shift will help protect our digital lives for decades to come.
