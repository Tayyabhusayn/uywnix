Advances in satellite technology and artificial intelligence are providing new ways to monitor and protect human rights. In 2026, these tools are being used more than ever to document events in remote or conflict-affected areas.
### Real-Time Monitoring
High-resolution satellite imagery allows organizations to track movements, identify destroyed infrastructure, and monitor the situation in places that are difficult for researchers to reach on the ground, such as parts of Myanmar.
### The Role of AI in Analysis
AI systems can now process thousands of satellite images in seconds, looking for specific patterns or changes that might indicate a human rights violation. This speed is essential for providing timely information to the international community.
### Challenges and Ethics
While these tools are powerful, they also raise questions about privacy and the risk of misuse. Organizations are working to develop ethical guidelines to ensure that this technology is used solely for the protection of vulnerable populations.
