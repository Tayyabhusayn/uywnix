For years, the Rohingya people have been living in temporary camps, primarily in Bangladesh. As we move into 2026, their situation remains a critical human rights issue that requires global attention.
### Life in the Camps
Over a million refugees live in places like Cox's Bazar. While these camps provide safety from violence, they are overcrowded. Families live in shelters made of bamboo and plastic, facing risks from monsoon rains and fires.
### The Challenge of Repatriation
Many Rohingya want to go back to their homes in Myanmar, but only if they are guaranteed safety and citizenship. Currently, the conditions in Myanmar are not considered safe enough for a large-scale return.
### Seeking Solutions
International groups are focusing on education and skill-building within the camps to help refugees prepare for the future. However, the ultimate goal remains a political solution that allows these people to live with dignity and rights.
