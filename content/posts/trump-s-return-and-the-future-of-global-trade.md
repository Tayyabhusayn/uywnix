The global economy is preparing for significant changes as Donald Trump returns to the political spotlight. For international trade, this means a likely shift back toward protectionist policies and new tariffs.
### Key Impacts on Trade
Analysts expect a renewed focus on "America First" policies. This could involve higher taxes on imported goods, especially from major trading partners like China and Europe. The goal is to encourage manufacturing within the United States, but it often leads to higher prices for consumers.
### Geopolitical Shifts
Beyond the US, countries are already looking for ways to protect their own economies. We may see new trade agreements forming between nations that want to reduce their dependence on American markets.
### What to Watch
The next few months will be critical. Businesses are keeping a close eye on policy announcements to adjust their supply chains. In simple terms, the "stable" trade environment we have seen recently is about to become much more dynamic.
