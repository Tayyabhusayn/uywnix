The return of Donald Trump to the US presidency has brought renewed focus on the NATO alliance. His "America First" approach is once again sparking a debate about the future of transatlantic security.
### Sharing the Burden
A major theme of the current administration is the demand for European nations to increase their defense spending. This has led to a flurry of new military investments across the continent as countries seek to prove their commitment to the alliance.
### Strategic Autonomy
In response to uncertainty about US policy, many European leaders are calling for "strategic autonomy"—the ability for Europe to defend itself without relying solely on the United States. This shift is leading to closer military cooperation within the European Union.
### The Importance of Unity
Despite the tensions, both sides recognize that a strong and united NATO is essential for global stability. The next few years will be a test of whether the alliance can adapt to a changing political landscape while remaining effective.
