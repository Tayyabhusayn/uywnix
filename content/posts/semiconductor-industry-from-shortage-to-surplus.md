The semiconductor industry, which powers everything from smartphones to electric cars, is undergoing a major shift. After years of shortages, the global market is now seeing a significant increase in supply.
### Increased Manufacturing Capacity
In response to previous shortages, many countries built new "fabs" or factories. In 2026, these factories are coming online, leading to a much higher volume of chips entering the market.
### Economic Impact
While more chips are good for manufacturers, it has led to lower prices and higher competition. Companies are now focusing on specialized chips for AI and green energy rather than general-purpose components.
### Future Outlook
The focus for the next few years will be on innovation in "beyond silicon" materials. Researchers are looking for ways to make chips even smaller and more energy-efficient to meet the demands of next-generation technology.
